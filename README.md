# Swiss AI Weeks
This project houses the UBS supported Swiss-AI-Weeks hackathon projects and associated material

[Swiss{ai}Weeks](https://ai-weeks.ch/) brings together developers, entrepreneurs, and citizens to help shape the future of AI through hands-on collaboration, open exchange, and a strong commitment to ethical and trustworthy AI, please visit the website for more details and events.

UBS is proud to both [partner](https://ai-weeks.ch/2026/partners/ubs) with Swiss {ai} Weeks and to offer up interesting challenges, continuing our commitment to support Swiss innovation.
